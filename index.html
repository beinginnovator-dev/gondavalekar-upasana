About Shri Gondavalekar Maharaj

Birth & Early Life

Shri Brahmachaitanya Gondavalekar Maharaj was born on 19 February 1845 (Magha Shuddha Dwadashi) at Gondavale Budruk, Satara district, Maharashtra. His birth name was Ganpati (Ganu) Raoji Ghugardare. Born into a Deshastha Brahmin family devoted to Lord Vitthala, he showed deep spiritual inclination from childhood.

Guru & Initiation

At a young age he left home in search of a Guru. He met Shri Tukamai (Tukaram Chaitanya) at Yehalegaon near Nanded. After nine months of intense service and tests, on Ram Navami he was initiated with the Trayodashakshari mantra "Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram" and given the spiritual name Brahmachaitanya.

Householder Saint

Following his Guru's guidance, he lived as a householder while continuously practicing and teaching Naam Japa. He built many Rama temples across Maharashtra, Karnataka and beyond. He emphasized that true devotion does not require renouncing the world — one can attain God while fulfilling family duties with constant remembrance of Rama.

Samadhi

Shri Maharaj took Samadhi on 22 December 1913 (Margashirsha Vadya Dashami) at Gondavale. His Samadhi Mandir continues to be a major centre of Ram Naam Japa, Annadan and Upasana. Every year the Punyatithi Mahotsav is celebrated with great devotion.

Upasana Details

What is Upasana?

Upasana means "sitting near" or worship with deep devotion. In the tradition of Gondavalekar Maharaj, Upasana centres on three pillars:

  • Naam Smaran — Continuous chanting of Ram Naam
  • Sagunopasana — Worship of the divine form (idol/samadhi)
  • Annadan — Feeding all without discrimination

Essence of Worship

Maharaj taught that the Japam of the Divine Name means awareness of the Divine presence and surrender of one's ego to His Will. Every year a resolution (sankalp) is made to complete 13 crore Ram Naam Japam. Devotees contribute from home and at Gondavale.

Daily Practice for Devotees

  • Chant "Shri Ram Jai Ram Jai Jai Ram" as much as possible throughout the day
  • Perform morning and evening remembrance of Maharaj / Rama
  • Maintain purity in thought, word and deed
  • Serve others selflessly (especially Annadan when possible)
  • Attend satsang and read Maharaj's pravachane / bodhavachane
  • Keep the mind on Rama even while doing worldly work

Key Teaching

"Mukhī Bhagavantāche Nām | Hātī Prapanchāche Kām | Aisā Jodā Manī Rām"

(Divine Name on the lips, worldly duties in the hands, and Rama in the mind)
